I'm a Ph.D. candidate in the [Robot Learning Lab (RLLab)](https://rllab.snu.ac.kr/) at Seoul National University, advised by Professor [Songhwai Oh](https://rllab.snu.ac.kr/people/songhwai-oh). Before starting my Ph.D., I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University in 2020.

My research interests lie broadly in **robot learning**, with a particular focus on **vision-language-action (VLA) models**, **high-level planning**, and **humanoid robotics**.
